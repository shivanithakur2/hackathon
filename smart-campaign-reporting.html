Understanding ROAS
What ROAS means
ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) tells you how much sales your campaign generated for every $1 spent on marketing — including discounts and DoorDash fees.
Example: A ROAS of 5.0x means you made $5 in sales for every $1 in spend.
Why Smart Campaign ROAS looks different
Smart Campaigns optimize for incremental growth, not just efficiency.

A very high ROAS often means your campaign reached customers who were likely to order anyway.

A moderate or lower ROAS can actually be healthier — it means your campaign is reaching new or lapsed customers and driving true sales growth over time.
What else to track
ROAS is just one part of the story. Check these metrics together for a full view of success:

Net Sales Lift: Sales minus promo spend — best sign of profitable growth.

Customer Mix: Growth in new and lapsed customers = strong incrementality.

Take-Home Pay: Shows how Smart Campaigns improve your actual earnings.
