Understanding ROAS
What is ROAS?
Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) measures the revenue generated for every dollar spent on advertising or marketing campaigns.
How to read it
Example: A ROAS of 6.12x means:
For every $1 spent → You earned $6.12 in sales
For every $100 spent → You earned $612 in sales
What's a good ROAS?
Less than 1x
You're losing money. For every dollar spent, you're earning less than a dollar back.
1x to 3x
Breaking even to moderate return. Your campaign is covering costs but could be optimized.
Greater than 3x
Excellent return! Your campaign is performing very well and generating strong profits.
Tips to improve ROAS
• Target your most valuable customers
• Test different promotional offers
• Optimize campaign timing
• Refine your audience segments
